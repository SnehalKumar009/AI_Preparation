# Nimbus Robotics: Company Overview

Nimbus Robotics builds autonomous sidewalk delivery robots for grocery chains.
Founded in 2021 in Pune, the company has 140 employees across Pune (engineering),
Rotterdam (European operations) and Austin (US pilot).

Flagship product: the **Courier C2**, a six-wheeled robot that carries up to
18 kg for up to 6 km. The C2 replaced the older C1 in January of this year.

Key customers: FreshCart (India, 900 robots), Albers Markt (Netherlands, 220
robots), and a 40-robot pilot with Lone Star Grocers in Austin.

Leadership: CEO Meera Iyer, CTO Daan de Vries, VP Operations Carlos Mendez.
