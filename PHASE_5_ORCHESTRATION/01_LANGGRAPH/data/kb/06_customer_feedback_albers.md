# Customer Feedback: Albers Markt (from the 12 August steering call)

- Albers Markt is satisfied with the 5 Rotterdam stores already live on C2.
- Their main concern: the 4 Utrecht stores planned for September, because
  their autumn marketing campaign starts 1 October.
- They would accept a phased go-live if at least 8 of the 12 stores are live
  by 30 September and the rest by 31 October.
- They asked for a written briefing with a realistic date per store group.
