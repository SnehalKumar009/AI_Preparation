# Incident Report IR-311: VoltCell battery pack shortage

Date opened: 22 July. Status: mitigated, not resolved.

VoltCell, our only qualified supplier of C2 battery packs, cut deliveries by
40% after a fire at its Brno plant. Only 72 of the 120 packs due on 1 September
will arrive; the remaining 48 are expected mid-October.

Impact: without packs, 48 robots cannot ship. The Q3 Albers Markt rollout
will reach at most 132 of 180 robots by 30 September.

Mitigation so far: 20 packs borrowed from the FreshCart spares pool in Pune
(approved by the CEO on 2 August). A second supplier, Kestrel Power, is in
qualification; earliest qualification date is 15 November.
