# Founder Profile
Founder Profile: Alex Vance Serial Entrepreneurship

## Alex Vance - Career
Silicon Valley veteran Alex Vance has maintained an incredibly prolific career over the last two decades, successfully launching five distinct technology companies. 

## Alex Vance - entrepreneurial journey
His entrepreneurial journey began with Vertex Data, an analytics tool that was eventually acquired by a larger enterprise. Following that exit, Vance founded Nexus Security, which grew steadily before also being acquired in a private transaction. 

## Alex Vance - public market debut
Seeking a public market debut for his next venture, Vance founded Horizon Cloud, which successfully completed an IPO and is currently listed on the NASDAQ exchange. His two most recent ventures, Apex AI and Catalyst Robotics, currently remain independent, early-stage private entities operating in the automation and deep-tech sectors.
