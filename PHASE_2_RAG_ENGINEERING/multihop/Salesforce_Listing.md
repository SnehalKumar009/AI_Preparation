# Corporate History of Salesforce Inc.
Corporate History of Salesforce Inc.

## Salesforce intro
Salesforce Inc. is a global leader in cloud-based customer relationship management software. The company was pioneered by former Oracle executive Marc Benioff in a small San Francisco apartment in March 1999. 

## Salesforce history
The company grew rapidly by introducing the revolutionary Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business model to the technology industry. After achieving massive market adoption and scaling its subscription revenues, Salesforce officially went public on the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker symbol CRM in 2004. 

## Salesforce footprint
Over the subsequent decades, Salesforce expanded its market footprint by migrating into enterprise security, collaborative software tools, and data analytics architectures.
