# Q3 Rollout Plan: Courier C2 for Albers Markt

Goal: deploy 180 additional C2 robots across 12 Albers Markt stores in
Rotterdam and Utrecht by 30 September.

Milestones:
- 15 July: 60 robots delivered from the Pune assembly line
- 10 August: city permit for Utrecht sidewalks
- 1 September: remaining 120 robots delivered
- 30 September: all 12 stores live

Dependencies: battery packs from supplier VoltCell (lead time 6 weeks);
Utrecht municipality permit; software release 4.2 with the new curb-climbing
controller.

Owner: Carlos Mendez (VP Operations). Budget: EUR 2.1M.
