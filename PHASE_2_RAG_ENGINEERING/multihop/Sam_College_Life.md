# Stanford Alumini Spot Light
Stanford Alumni Spotlight 2012

## Early Years
The mid-2000s at Stanford University served as a legendary incubator for modern tech founders. Our archives show that in the autumn of 2004, a young Sam Altman moved into Room 314 of the Wilbur Hall dormitory. 

## Academic Focus
His assigned undergraduate roommate for that academic year was Alex Vance, a brilliant computer science major from Ohio. While Sam focused early on location-based mobile applications, Alex spent his late-night dorm hours building custom compiler tools and distributed database prototypes. 

## Friendship
The two roommates frequently collaborated on hackathons, establishing a competitive friendship that lasted long after they both eventually exited the campus ecosystem to pursue full-time venture-backed entrepreneurship in Silicon Valley.
