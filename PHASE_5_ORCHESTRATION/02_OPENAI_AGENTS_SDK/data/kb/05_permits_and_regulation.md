# Permits and Regulation: Netherlands

Rotterdam: sidewalk operation permit granted in March, valid for 3 years.

Utrecht: permit application submitted 20 June. The municipality approved it
on 8 August (two days ahead of plan) with one condition: robots must not
operate on the Oudegracht wharf paths between 11:00 and 15:00.

National: the RDW (Dutch vehicle authority) requires an incident log to be
filed within 72 hours of any collision. No collisions have been reported
this year.
