# Salesforce
TechCrunch: Salesforce Signals Intent to Buy Nexus Security

## Salesforce purchase
Salesforce has officially agreed to purchase cloud-native compliance platform Nexus Security for an undisclosed cash sum, continuing its aggressive expansion into the enterprise security ecosystem. 

## Nexus Security
Nexus Security, founded by serial entrepreneur Alex Vance, specializes in zero-trust architecture and automated compliance auditing for multi-cloud environments. 

## Salesforce roadmap
Salesforce plans to absorb the entire engineering team of Nexus Security. This move allows Salesforce to embed cutting-edge defensive layers directly into its core CRM architecture to appease highly regulated banking and healthcare clients.
